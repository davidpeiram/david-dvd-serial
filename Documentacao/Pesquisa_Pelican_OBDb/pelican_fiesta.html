Ford Fiesta OBD-II support

Model years 1996 to 2023CombustionOnboarded

83,036 miles driven with Pelican by 81 drivers

133,633 kilometers driven with Pelican by 81 drivers

The OBDb lists 203 parameters for the Ford Fiesta, read from 6 control modules. Real responses from Ford Fiesta cars confirm 121 of the parameters. Drivers have tested model years 2011, 2013 to 2022.

    Where is the Ford Fiesta's OBD-II port?

    The OBD-II port is usually under the dashboard, between the steering column and the door. Some cars put it in the center console or behind a cover.
